04 — experience
2025 — 2026
PFA Intern — Finished Goods Logistics
INGELEC
Diagnosed and modeled the finished-goods order-preparation process (Magasin Produits Finis), targeting the lead time between the SAP Bon de Prélèvement and Bon de Livraison generation.
  • Applied a full DMAIC approach on ~21,600 order lines, isolating warehouse congestion — not order size — as the dominant lead-time driver.
  • Built a 3D scenario simulation (FlexSim) and a Python discrete-event model (SimPy) calibrated against real SAP data, then extended it into a Monte Carlo scenario engine (Streamlit) to test root-cause levers before recommending changes.
  • Tested an arrival-smoothing scenario showing a measurable improvement, and reported a negative/null result (FIFO vs. SPT) alongside it rather than only the favorable finding.
